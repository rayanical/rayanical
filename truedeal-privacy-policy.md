# TrueDeal Privacy Policy

TrueDeal compares prices on supported Amazon.com product pages with price history supplied by the Keepa browser extension.

## Information used

On supported product pages, TrueDeal reads the product identifier, displayed price, and delivery information needed to determine whether it can show a comparison. It reads historical price data from Keepa's embedded chart. TrueDeal stores your comparison settings in browser local storage and holds price history temporarily in extension memory. It uses this information only to provide the price-comparison feature.

## Sharing

TrueDeal processes this information in your browser. TrueDeal does not send it to a TrueDeal server, use analytics, or sell it. Amazon and Keepa operate independently and have their own privacy practices; this policy does not govern their services or extensions.

TrueDeal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

You can change comparison settings from the TrueDeal popup. Removing TrueDeal through your browser's extension settings deletes its stored settings. Closing the browser clears history held in extension memory.
